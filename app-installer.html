🔐 Sign an IPA with your own Apple certificate

Use the existing MinzSign backend to re-sign an app you own or are permitted to distribute. You need a matching .p12 certificate, .mobileprovision profile and certificate password. A website cannot create valid Apple signing credentials for free.

Private-key warning: A .p12 contains sensitive signing keys. Signing uploads these files and the password to your configured server, where they must be decrypted temporarily. Use only your own trusted backend. Files are not sent while the backend is unconfigured.
