Important to know

Spotify Developer Mode currently requires the app owner to have Spotify Premium and allows up to five authorised users. This is suitable for private testing, not an unlimited public music service.

Your Spotify password is entered on Spotify's own website, not into Minzify. This integration does not request a Client Secret.

Full songs: Spotify's API does not provide raw free-to-stream song files. Results open in Spotify; they do not play through the Minzify Audius player.

Setup: Create or open your Spotify Developer app, select Web API, add the Redirect URI shown on this page, save your Client ID, then connect.
